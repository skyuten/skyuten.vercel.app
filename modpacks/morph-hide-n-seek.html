Minecraft : Morph Hide N' Seek Modpack

This is a near 1:1 recreation of PopularMMOs' Morph Hide N' Seek experience, with most of the mods they used for their videos and 6 of 6 major maps they used for their videos.

MODS INCLUDE:

  • Morph - iChun
  • Animania (Whole group of mods) - Purplicious_Cow_
  • Animal Bikes - Noppes_
  • Extra Golems - skyjay1
  • Twilight Forest - Benimatic
  • Orespawn - TheyCallMeDanger
  • SoggyMustache's Transportation - SoggyStache
  • Not Enough Items 1.8+ (Unofficial) - covers1624
  • Damage Indicators - rich1051414
  • Hide Names - thislooksfun1 (use command: /name option default [on/off])
  • e4mc retro - xhyrom

WORLDS:

  • So Kawwai! - By MerryRainbow (Modified by Skyuten)
  • CandyLand - By StaleDonut (Modified by Skyuten)
  • Accurate Jen's House (Actual World!) Hide N' Seek Version - By DehEpicOof
  • Village Hide N' Seek - By Skyuten
  • Jen's Giant House Hide N' Seek Version - By Skyuten
  • Zoo Map - By TestedMods (Modified by Skyuten)

1. [If forge auto install doesn't work] Install the version of Forge that corresponds with the modpack (http://files.minecraftforge.net/ Choose the installer version of Forge) Eg. Choose the latest 1.12.2 version of Forge if the modpack is in 1.12.2.

2. Download the Modpack

3. Choose 'import profile/instance' option on launcher and choose the downloaded zip file

4. Open Minecraft and make sure your profile is set to Forge

5. Play!
